02 Artist & Business Right to Refuse Service (Proper Advance Timing)

Events, venues, and schedules can change unexpectedly. To protect both the host and GG Loop LLC cleanly:

  • Pre-Event Right to Decline (Advance Notice Window): GG Loop LLC / DJ JRIP reserves the right to refuse, decline, or step back from any booking prior to the event date if venue power/safety checks fail, event scope changes materially from the approved brief, payment/chargeback fraud signals arise, or a documented emergency / scheduling conflict occurs.
  • Proper Timing Commitment: Non-emergency pre-event declines will be communicated promptly in writing as soon as discovered (targeting at least 7+ days’ advance notice whenever feasible so the host has fair lead time).
  • 100% Retainer Refund Protection: If DJ JRIP / GG Loop LLC declines or cancels a booking prior to performance due to scheduling conflict, illness, or Force Majeure (and not due to client payment fraud/chargeback or on-site refusal after arrival), 100% of your paid retainer is refunded to the original payment method within 3–5 business days. Total liability of GG Loop LLC under any circumstance is strictly limited to amounts actually paid by the Client.

03 On-Site Venue Safety, Weather Shield & Conduct Termination

  • Safe Workspace & Power: Client/Venue guarantees a clean, level, covered surface (minimum 6ft × 4ft) protected from rain, lawn sprinklers, direct scorching sun, and drink-splash zones, plus a dedicated grounded 15A–20A electrical circuit within 25 ft of the booth.
  • Zero Harassment / Gear Protection: If a guest threatens violence, physically tampers with equipment, spills liquids toward live decks/PA electronics, or creates an unsafe electrical/weather hazard after notice is given to the host, DJ JRIP reserves the right to immediately cease performance and pack out with zero refund of paid retainer or event fees.
